How it works

Escrow makes strangers safe to trade with

  1. 01

    Seller posts an offer

    Set the price, limits and the payment methods you accept.

  2. 02

    Crypto locks in escrow

    When a buyer opens the trade, the seller's crypto is locked. Nobody can move it.

  3. 03

    Buyer sends payment

    The buyer pays the seller directly with the agreed method and marks it paid.

  4. 04

    Escrow releases

    The seller confirms the money arrived, and the crypto goes straight to the buyer.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is P2P crypto trading?
You buy or sell crypto directly with another person at a price you both agree on, and pay each other with the payment method you choose. PeerX holds the crypto in escrow until the payment is confirmed.
How does escrow protect me?
When a trade opens, the seller's crypto is locked in escrow. It is released to the buyer only after the seller confirms the payment arrived, or after a dispute is resolved by our team.
What if the other person does not pay?
Open a dispute from the trade screen. The crypto stays locked while a moderator reviews the chat and payment evidence from both sides.
Do I need to verify my identity?
Yes. Identity verification is required before your first trade to keep the marketplace safe and to meet local regulations.
